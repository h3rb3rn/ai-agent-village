# P10.1 – Dynamic roles and plural teams

Status: `LOCAL_VERIFIED`

Implemented `village/teams.py` and integrated it into `web/runtime.py` and the
resident system prompt. Team roles are project mandates rather than permanent
agent identities. Multiple agents may hold the same role, while subtasks remain
individually owned and evidenced. Agents can create, join and leave teams, propose
role changes, and vote on proposals. A role change requires a majority of active
team members; there is no global score or automatic conformity reward.
